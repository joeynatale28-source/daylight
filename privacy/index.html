Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Daylight is a free web calendar app operated from Virginia, USA, by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information Daylight collects, how it is used and shared, and the rights you have over your data. It covers the Daylight website and the Daylight app. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use Daylight.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information. Your email address, display name, profile photo, birthday, and sign-in method (email and password, Google sign-in, or passkey).
  • Content you create. Your calendars, events, tasks, and settings. Your calendar content is yours.
  • Sharing data. Who you share calendars and events with, invitations you send, and public links you create.
  • AI-access data. Which AI services you connect to Daylight, the permissions you grant them (read, write, or delete), and a log of the actions they take on your calendars.
  • Technical data. Device and browser information, sign-in session records, and other information needed to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use information

We use your information to:

  • Operate the service — accounts, calendars, events, tasks, syncing, sharing, and notifications.
  • Send account emails, such as confirmation and password-reset messages. Daylight sends no marketing email.
  • Keep the service secure, prevent abuse, provide support, and comply with the law.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell your personal data. Your calendar content is never used to train AI models.

4. How information is shared

  • With people you choose. When you share a calendar or an event, or create a public link, the people you share with can see what you shared. Sharing is under your control and you can revoke it at any time.
  • With service providers. We use the following providers, each of which receives only the information it needs to perform its service: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage); GitHub Pages (website and app hosting); TomTom (location search queries, only when you type in the Location field); Google (only if you use Google sign-in); and email delivery through Supabase.
  • For legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Daylight, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer. If Daylight is acquired or merged, your information may transfer as part of that transaction, and we will notify you before it does.

5. Cookies and local storage

Daylight uses only strictly necessary cookies and local storage: keeping you signed in and remembering your preferences, such as your theme. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings; if you do, you will be signed out and your preferences will reset.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete an event, task, or calendar, it is removed from your account. When your account is deleted, your account data is deleted. Backups age out within 90 days. To delete your account, submit a data request (see Section 8) or contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

7. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls, and scoped AI-access tokens that you can revoke at any time. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

You control your data in the app: edit or delete your content, revoke shares and public links, disconnect AI access, and export your calendars as .ics files.

You also have the following legal rights:

  • Access. Confirm whether we process your personal data and get a copy of it.
  • Correction. Correct inaccuracies in your personal data.
  • Deletion. Delete your personal data.
  • Portability. Receive a portable copy of your personal data.
  • Opt-out. Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. Daylight does none of these three things, so these opt-outs are honored by design.

How to exercise your rights. Request your data or email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We verify requests against your account — you may be asked to sign in or confirm from your account email. We respond within 45 days, extendable once by 45 days with notice.

Appeals. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to the denial. We respond to appeals in writing within 60 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Virginia Attorney General.

California residents. Under the CCPA/CPRA, you have the rights to know, access, delete, correct, and receive a portable copy of your personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, so no opt-out link is needed. We use sensitive personal information only to run the service. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Authorized-agent requests are accepted with verification.

Other states. Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws — including Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Utah, and Iowa — have the same access, correction, deletion, and portability rights, through the same request process.

We do not charge for requests, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

9. Children's privacy

Daylight is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are for users 13 and older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete the account and its data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].
