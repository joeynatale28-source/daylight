Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Daylight ("Daylight," "we," "us," or "our") is a free calendar application that runs in your web browser at daylightcal.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Daylight, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have. By using Daylight, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run Daylight:

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored in hashed form — we never store your plain-text password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile photo from Google. If you use a passkey, we store only the public credential needed to verify your sign-in.
  • Profile information: a display name or profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Calendar content: the calendars, events, tasks, reminders, and notification preferences you create, and the settings for your calendars (including colors and default reminders).
  • Sharing and AI access: the sharing permissions you set on calendars and events, and the AI access grants you create (which assistant, and whether it may read, write, or delete).
  • Subscriptions: the addresses (URLs) of external calendars you subscribe to, and the calendar data fetched from them.
  • Place searches: the text you type when searching for a place, which is sent to our place-search provider (TomTom) to return matching places.
  • Technical information: basic information needed to operate and secure the service, such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of your visits.
  • Cookies and local storage: Daylight stores a session token so you stay signed in, and your preferences (such as theme, default calendar view, and time format). We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Messages you send us: if you contact us or submit a data request, we keep the information you provide so we can respond.

We do not collect payment information. Daylight is free, and we never ask for a card.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Daylight, including syncing your calendars across your devices.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Send transactional emails, such as sign-in verification, password resets, and data exports you request.
  • Respond to your requests, including data requests and support questions.
  • Protect the security of the service and investigate abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve the service.

We do not use your calendar content for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How we share your information

We share your information only as described here:

  • Service providers: we use trusted providers to run Daylight — Supabase (database, authentication, and backend hosting), Resend (email delivery), TomTom (place search), and GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (website hosting and DNS). They process your information only to provide their services to us.
  • AI assistants you connect: if you connect Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini through Daylight's AI access, that assistant can access your calendar data only within the permissions you grant (read, write, or delete). You can disconnect an assistant at any time in Settings, which immediately ends its access.
  • People you share with: if you share a calendar or an event, the people you share with can see what you chose to share, with the permissions you set. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of people, the security of the service, or our legal rights.
  • Business transfer: if Daylight is acquired or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner, and we will notify you.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies and local storage

Daylight uses cookies and local storage only for what the service needs:

  • Strictly necessary: your sign-in session and security.
  • Preferences: your theme, default view, time format, and similar settings.

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Daylight only uses strictly necessary cookies and local storage — your sign-in session, security, and preferences — so no cookie consent banner is needed. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings; doing so will sign you out.

6. Data retention

We keep your account information and calendar content while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your calendars, events, tasks, and settings. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short period after deletion and are then deleted. If you ask us to delete specific information, we delete it as described in Section 7.

7. Your rights and data requests

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format (JSON for your account data, ICS for your calendars).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights, use the Support Center (signed in), or email us at privacy@daylightcal.com. Here is how a data request works:

  • You submit the request from your signed-in account (or from the email address on your account, if you email us).
  • We review the request to confirm it comes from you.
  • For a data export, we prepare your full data as a ZIP file (your account data in readable JSON files, your calendars as ICS files, and a readable summary) and send it to you in your support ticket chat. The download expires after 24 hours.
  • For a deletion request, we delete your data as described in Section 6 and confirm by email.

We respond to requests within 30 days (for requests under European law) or 45 days (for requests under California law). If we need more time, we will tell you why and how long we need. We will never charge you for a request or discriminate against you for making one. If we cannot verify that a request comes from you, we may ask for additional information or decline the request.

8. Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the rights described in Section 7, plus:

  • The right to know the categories of personal information we collect (identifiers such as your email address; internet activity such as usage information; geolocation information from place searches; and user content such as your calendars and events), the purposes for collecting it, and the categories of recipients (our service providers, AI assistants you connect, and people you share with).
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • The right to non-discrimination: we will not deny you the service, charge you different prices, or give you a different quality of service because you exercised your rights.
  • You may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf; we will ask the agent for proof of your authorization.

To exercise these rights, use the Support Center or email privacy@daylightcal.com.

9. Your European privacy rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Our legal bases for processing your information are: performance of our contract with you (running Daylight), your consent (for example, when you connect an AI assistant or accept cookies), our legitimate interests (security and improving the service), and legal obligations.
  • You have the rights in Section 7, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • Your information is processed in the United States, where our providers host it. When we transfer information outside your region, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including the standard contractual clauses used by our providers.

10. Children

Daylight is for people aged 13 and older. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Daylight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete the account and its data. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has created an account, contact us at privacy@daylightcal.com and we will remove it.

11. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest through our hosting providers, and access controls that limit who can reach your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password and sign-in methods safe.

12. International transfers

Daylight is operated from the United States, and your information is processed there. If you use Daylight from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when the policy was last revised. Continued use of Daylight after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@daylightcal.com.

See also our Terms of Service.
