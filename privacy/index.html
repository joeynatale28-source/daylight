Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

Daylight ("Daylight," "we," "us," "our") is a calendar app at daylightcal.com. Daylight includes the website, the web app, the Daylight iOS app, and the Daylight Android app when it becomes available. If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the Support Center in your account (Settings → Support Center).

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information Daylight collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers everything you do in Daylight: your account, your calendars, events, and tasks, your settings, your support tickets, and your use of sign-in methods including email and password, Google sign-in, passkeys, and Face ID. It does not cover other websites or services you reach through links in Daylight, such as our documentation site or our status page — those have their own policies.

3. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, your email address, and your password (stored as a hash, never in plain text). You may also add a profile photo and a birthday in your profile. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, your email address, and your profile picture with us so we can create and identify your account.

Your content. Everything you put into Daylight is yours: your calendars, events, tasks, reminders, notifications, and any photos you attach to a support ticket. We store it so the service works and so it syncs between your devices — a change on your phone shows on the website, and a change on the website shows on your phone.

Sign-in information. If you use a passkey, your device stores the private key; we store only the public key needed to verify you. If you use Face ID, your biometric data is processed entirely on your device by Apple — Daylight never receives, stores, or sees it. If you sign in with Google, the sign-in request goes to Google, and Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can add or remove a sign-in method in Settings at any time.

Device and session information. We record the type of device and app you sign in with so we can show it in Settings under Active Sessions — for example, "Daylight iOS" for the iPhone app, "Daylight for Android" for the Android app, and the browser for the website. We also record when a session was last active so you can sign out of a device you no longer use.

Places searches. When you search for a place, your search text and your approximate location are sent to Mapbox so it can return results. Mapbox's handling of that information is governed by Mapbox's Privacy Policy.

Support communications. When you contact support or open a ticket in the Support Center, we collect the title, details, photos, and messages in that ticket, and any actions taken on it. If you request your data, we collect that request and the export we generate for it.

Security information. To keep accounts safe, we record sign-in attempts and security checks. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that sign-ins and sensitive actions are made by a person and not a bot; Turnstile may collect device and browser signals for that check.

What we do not collect. Daylight does not show ads and does not use advertising trackers. We do not collect your precise location except when you search for a place. We do not collect payment information — Daylight is free, with no trial and no card needed.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to provide Daylight and keep it working: to create and secure your account, to store and sync your calendars, events, and tasks, to show you notifications, to respond to support tickets and data requests, to protect the service from abuse and bots, and to comply with the law. We do not use your calendars, events, or tasks to train AI models, and we do not sell your information.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers Daylight needs to run, only for the purpose listed, and only the information that purpose needs:

  • Supabase — stores your account, calendars, events, tasks, and settings, and runs the sign-in, sync, and AI-access services.
  • Resend — sends Daylight's emails, such as password resets and sign-in notices.
  • Mapbox — provides place search and reverse geocoding.
  • Google — provides Google sign-in when you choose it.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the website and app, handles the domain, and runs the Turnstile security check.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website and app.
  • Apple — distributes the iOS app through the App Store. Apple does not receive your Daylight data from us.

If you connect an AI assistant (Claude or ChatGPT) in Settings under AI access, that assistant may read, write, or delete only what you allow it to, under the permissions you pick. You can see what it has done in Recent AI activity, and you can disconnect it at any time. We do not share your information with anyone else except when you ask us to (for example, when you share a calendar or an event with someone), or when the law requires it.

6. AI access

In Settings under AI access, you can connect Claude or ChatGPT to Daylight. When you connect one, you pick what it may do: read, write, or delete. The assistant acts only within those permissions, and only on your own data. Daylight records what connected assistants do in Recent AI activity so you can check it. You can disconnect an assistant at any time, and disconnecting stops its access immediately. Daylight does not send your data to any AI assistant you have not connected.

7. Cookies

Daylight uses only essential cookies: the ones needed to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and keep the service secure. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies, and there is no cookie banner.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as your account exists and as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. When you delete your account, your calendars, events, tasks, and files are deleted. Copies in backups may remain until those backups are overwritten. Support tickets are kept while they are needed to help you and to keep a record of what was done.

9. Your rights

If you live in California (CCPA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect about you and why, to ask us to delete it, to ask us to correct it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of it. Daylight does not sell your personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another place covered by the GDPR. You have the right to access your personal information, to correct it, to erase it, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, to restrict how we process it, and to object to our processing of it. Where we process your information with your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

How to exercise your rights. In Daylight, open the Support Center and create a data request ticket. For a data request, we will generate a full export of your data (your information as structured data, plus your calendars as files) and send it to you through your ticket; the download link expires after 24 hours. We respond to requests within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, so that we do not give your data to someone else.

10. Children

Daylight is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you are under 13, do not create an account. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created an account, contact us through the Support Center in that account and we will delete it.

11. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, with passwords stored only as hashes, with passkeys verified by public-key cryptography, and with access controls that limit who inside Daylight's systems can reach your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your information safe and we will tell you if we learn of a breach affecting your account.

12. International transfers

Daylight is based in the United States. Your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Where the law requires it, we rely on the providers' safeguards for those transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Links to other services

Daylight links to other services, including our documentation and our status page. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them. Check their policies before you share information with them.

14. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when Daylight changes — for example, when we add a sign-in method, a provider, or a feature. When we change it, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email. Continuing to use Daylight after a change means you accept the new policy.

15. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your information, go through the Support Center in your account (Settings → Support Center).

See also our Terms of Service.
