Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page will explain what information Daylight collects and how it is used.

Full legal text coming soon — the summaries below describe each section in plain language.

1. Introduction

Daylight is a calendar app. This policy explains what information Daylight collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

The account details you provide, the calendars, events, and tasks you create, and the connection details for any AI assistant you choose to link.

3. How we use information

To run the app: show your calendars, sync your events, send notifications, and let a connected AI assistant do what you asked.

4. How information is shared

Your calendars are private unless you share them, and sharing shows the other person only what you shared. Daylight does not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and local storage

Daylight uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings like your theme. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers.

6. Data retention
