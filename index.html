FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Daylight free?

Yes. Daylight is free to use.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Daylight runs in your browser, on a computer or a phone.

Who can see my calendars and tasks?

Only you, unless you choose to share a calendar or an event. They are saved to your own Daylight account.

How do I sign in?

With an email and password, with Google, or with a passkey.

How does AI access work?

In Settings, open AI access, connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with the link shown there, and pick what it may do: read, write or delete. You can disconnect it at any time.
