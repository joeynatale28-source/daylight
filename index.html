      Questions,
      answered

      Is Daylight free?

      Yes. Daylight is free to use, with no trial and no card needed. Create an account and everything on this page is yours.

      Do I need to install anything?

      No. Daylight runs in your browser, on a computer or a phone, so there is nothing to download or update. Open it, sign in, and your calendar is there.

      Who can see my calendars and tasks?

      Only you, unless you choose to share a calendar or an event. Everything is saved to your own Daylight account, and when you do share, you pick what each person can do and can stop sharing at any time.

      How do I sign in?

      With an email and password, with Google, or with a passkey. You can add or change a sign-in method later in Settings.

      How does AI access work?

      In Settings, open AI access, connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with the link shown there, and pick what it may do: read, write or delete. You can disconnect it at any time.
